Who We Are

SAP SADA SOCIAL WELFARE & LIFE PROTECTION UNIT

Together We Care. Together We Protect Life.

Headquartered at Jayabodhi Viharaya, Nalluruwa, Panadura (Registration No: G A 00282904), our unit is a dedicated humanitarian service entity in Sri Lanka. We operate on the direct ethos of unyielding public service: protecting human lives in high-risk aquatic zones, clearing heavily choked canals to mitigate floods, and providing economic resilience to vulnerable mothers and families.

Life Protection
Aquatic rescue training, safety patrols and coastal awareness.
Wetland Preservation
Heavy machinery dredging and plastic-free water systems.

Our Core Foundation

Guiding Principles Driving Our Community Service

Our Mission

To foster a caring, resilient, and empowered society by delivering dedicated life protection services, emergency support, environmental preservation, and social welfare programs rooted in transparency and community cooperation.

Our Vision

A thriving Sri Lanka where every human life is cherished and safeguarded, natural aquatic ecosystems are preserved, and every community thrives with dignity, solidarity, and mutual responsibility.
