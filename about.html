Who We Are

SAP SADA SOCIAL WELFARE & LIFE PROTECTION UNIT (Registration No: G A 00282904) is a non-governmental humanitarian organization established in Sri Lanka with a foundational commitment to public welfare, life preservation, community upliftment, and environmental responsibility.

Headquartered at Jayabodhi Viharaya, Nalluruwa, our unit works tirelessly on the ground with dedicated volunteers, public rescue responders, local communities, and municipal stakeholders to build a secure, resilient, and compassionate society.

Our Strategic Objectives

  • Enhance community safety and aquatic life-saving readiness across coastal and inland waterways.
  • Promote public health, environmental hygiene, and wetland restoration through direct collective intervention.
  • Empower vulnerable families, youth, and women through skill development, self-employment assistance, and welfare support.
  • Provide prompt humanitarian assistance during natural disasters, floods, and personal emergencies.
  • Advocate for human rights, civic welfare, and transparent community leadership.

Our Mission

To foster a caring, resilient, and empowered society by delivering dedicated life protection services, emergency support, environmental preservation, and social welfare programs rooted in transparency and community cooperation.

Our Vision

A thriving Sri Lanka where every human life is cherished and safeguarded, natural aquatic ecosystems are preserved, and every community thrives with dignity, solidarity, and mutual responsibility.

Our Core Values

Humanity, Transparency, Life Protection, Community Participation, and Integrity guide everything we do.
